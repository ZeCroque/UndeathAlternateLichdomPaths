## 1. OVERVIEW

Expand your path to lichdom with flexible ritual requirements, alternative quest progression, and support for custom lich ritual and infernal alchemy sites.

## 2. DETAILS

The mod has two main feature:
- Adding a new quickstart for the lich transformation with less narration. A freeform lich ritual or "Undeath Lite" if you prefer.
- Adding support for new ritual sites for other mods to implement. Currently only implemented by my [Soul Cairn Patches]() mod.

### Skipping Undeath Questline

With the mod installed, you can now head straight to Scourg Barrow to find the Black Book and unlock the ritual. There will be a new quest starting once you enter the tomb (with very few map markers), that will help you open the final gate. No need to investigate a Vigilant of Stendarr's caravan nor to chase a priest of Arkay for his heart anymore.

Once you read the black book, a messenger will lead you to the broker as usual.

There's also some misc quests that can help you find Scourg Barrow if you need:
- Enthir (College of Winterhold's broker) has a new dialog where you can ask him if he knows where you can learn more about necromancy.
- Valerica has a new dialog for the same thing once you defeated Harkon and she's back to her study.
- Neloth can lead you to the Black Book as well. It was "fixed" by Classical Lichdom but this mod reverses the change.
- A note will spawn in necromancer bosses' inventory with 33% chance once you reached level 30.
All these hint quest are configurable through MCM.

### Freeform Ritual

Now you might be wondering: "Ok, but If I skip the preliminary quests how do I get the Shroud of Vyngald and the poison?". Well, clever user, I got you covered:
- You will find an "Infernal Alchemy" cauldron inside the ritual sites added by the original mod (Solitude Sewers and Ravenscorn Spire) to create the "Embalming Essence" or "Namira's Corrosion", with the recipe and some empty vials next to it
- You can now use any Daedric artifact as a vessel for the ritual (including Auriel artifacts). When you activate the "Necromantic Altar", you will have the choice between crafting as usual or cursing an artifact. You can curse only once and the cursed artifact will be destroyed after the ritual is performed, so choose carefully!

## 6. INSTALLATION

**Automatic (Recommended)**
- Use the Mod Manager Download button. Install and enable the file(s) in your favorite mod manager (ModOrganizer2 is my personal preference).

**Manual**
- Extract the required files (cf. `5. MOD CONTENT`) from the archive to your Data folder and activate them in the in-game Creations menu.

## 7. COMPATIBILITY

**The "Immersive Start" option from Classical Lichdom must be activated to skip the main quest. You can turn it off however if you're only willing to use the custom ritual site feature.**
